The Apache Nutch PMC are pleased to announce the immediate release of Apache Nutch v1.23 , we advise all current users and developers of the 1.X series to upgrade to this release. Download Nutch 1.23 You may also be interested in the 1.23 release report and the breaking changes: Nutch requires JDK 17 at build and runtime. The minimum required Hadoop version is 3.5.0 if Nutch is run on a Hadoop cluster.
